Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our mobile application ("Knowledge Stack" or "the Game"). By using the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a persistent gaming experience, we collect the following limited data:

  • Device Identifiers: We collect anonymously generated device identifiers or platform-specific gaming IDs (like Google Play Games or Apple Game Center IDs) to recognize your account.
  • Game Progress Data: We store your current level, total score, and the IDs of the questions you have successfully answered to ensure you can resume the game from where you left off.

We do not collect personal identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To save and synchronize your game progress across sessions.
  • To display your correct score and level within the application.
  • To ensure you do not receive the exact same questions repeatedly once solved.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your game progress data is stored securely on our servers. We employ industry-standard security measures, including rate limiting and encrypted API communication, to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Third-Party Services

At this time, our Game does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect data independently. If we integrate services like Google Play Games Services or Apple Game Center for authentication, your data will also be subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Game is a trivia and puzzle game designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Given the anonymous nature of our user identification (Device IDs), we cannot identify the age of our users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
